# Magian Moogle trials

The **Magian Moogle** card on the Data page runs the Trial of the Magians
catalogue: every trial item with its immediate trial steps. Pick a step and the
copy in the bags is replaced by the trial's reward, with the Magian block
(`kind 0x02`, sub-kind `0x43`, four augment words plus the trial id) written in
the same operation. The client reads the block back on `//reload`.

## Where the data comes from

`data/magian.json` is generated by `tools/gen_magian.py` from two sources:

- **LandSandBoat's `scripts/globals/magian_data.lua`**, the structured trial
  table: 759 trials over 798 items, covering the classic level-75 weapon chains
  (Peeler, Chopper, ...), the Relic/Mythic 90/95/99 upgrades and the Empyrean
  and Relic armour `+1`/`+2` steps. Each trial gives the required item, the
  reward item, the reward's augment words and the trial id. The item names and
  slots come from the local `items` table.
- **BG Wiki's trial category pages** for the i119 augment weapons
  ([Category:Trial of the Magians](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Category:Trial_of_the_Magians)).
  Centovente, Fusetto `+1`/`+2`/`+3`, Machaera, Thibron, Uzura and the other
  final weapons of the Dagger, Sword, Katana, Archery and Marksmanship pages
  keep their item id and choose one of three augment blocks; the wiki gives the
  trial number and the row text, and the generator maps the text to the client
  ids (DMG 0x2E4, Weapon Skill Damage 0x147, TP Bonus 0x161, Store TP 0x08E,
  Delay 0x2F9, ...).

The augment ids use the client's **Magian scaling**, which differs from the
cape/Moonshade table: `0x161` value 19 is TP Bonus +1000 (`(19 + 1) * 50`), and
`0x2E4` value 0 is DMG +1. `server/fanxi_server/game/magian.py` keeps that table
and the block codec.

## The card

- **Filters**: name, Weapons/Armor and **Owned only** (on by default). The
  catalogue has 597 items with trials; the select caps at 400 rows and asks for
  a narrower filter if more match.
- **Paths**: each immediate trial is a card. It says `Trial N` and either
  `→ Reward` (the item changes) or `augment` (the same item takes the block).
  The augment rows and the objective count are shown; **Complete trial** writes
  the step.
- Copies in the inventory, wardrobe and storage are all listed and can be
  upgraded in place. A preview of an unowned item disables the button and points
  at the Armory.
- After a trial that changes the item, the card selects the reward item so the
  next step is one click away. The block's `current` text comes from
  `magian.describe`, which uses the same table as the stat sheet.

Retail's trial progress (insignia, objective tracking, the ten-trial limit) is
not simulated; the card is a way to skip to the reward state.

## Coverage notes

- The i119 augment tables on the Hand-to-Hand, Great Sword, Great Axe, Scythe,
  Polearm, Great Katana, Club, Staff, Instrument and Shield pages do not use the
  wiki's `{{augment}}` template, so their final augment weapons are not in the
  JSON yet; their classic chains and the Relic/Mythic stages are (those come from
  LandSandBoat).
- The Alabaster/Murky Furnace gear is a separate card (see
  [limbus-r30.md](limbus-r30.md)).

## Tests

`server/tests/test_magian.py` covers the block codec, the catalogue, a chain
step that replaces the item, an augment-only step, Centovente's TP Bonus path,
invalid trials and the stat-sheet read; `test_api.py` covers the HTTP operation.
