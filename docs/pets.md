# Pets

Fanxi has a small pet foundation on top of the entity model the Trusts already use.

## What exists

- **Call Beast** (BST) spawns the pet named by the jug in the ammo slot and spends one
  jug. **Bestial Loyalty** spawns the same pet without spending the jug.  A missing jug
  is reported before the pet is called.  The 97 jugs come from
  `data/jug_pets.json` (generated by `tools/gen_jug_pets.py` from LandSandBoat's
  `item_weapon.subskill` -> `pet_list` -> `mob_pools`/`pet_skills`; regenerate with
  `tools/gen_jug_pets.py --source /tmp/lsb_full`).
- **Call Wyvern** (DRG) spawns a wyvern.
- **Activate** (PUP) spawns an automaton.
- **Summon** spells spawn their avatar server-side (Carbuncle, Fenrir, Ifrit, Titan,
  Leviathan, Garuda, Shiva, Ramuh, Diabolos, Alexander, Odin).  Carbuncle and Ifrit are
  verified in the client, including the Summoner main-job UI.
- **Leave / Release / Dismiss / Deactivate** remove the active pet.  **Leave** is the BST
  pet-command version and says so in chat.
- **Fight / Sic / Ready** put the pet into fight mode; **Heel** follows; **Stay** holds position.
- **Tame** disengages a mob only the Beastmaster has claimed, or leaves an unclaimed mob
  "friendlier" for the next **Charm** (+10 percentage points).  Tame can miss: 75% base plus
  the equipped Tame success rate (Totemic Helm +4 is +31, so it is certain with that helm).
- **Gauge** reports the target's HP percentage and a charmability read.  **Charm** turns a
  mob into a simple charmed pet.
- Pets follow their owner, close on the owner's engaged target, swing with the existing
  combat roll, and pass half their damage as owner hate.
- The three models come from the retail pet pools: Tiger familiar, player wyvern and
  automaton.  The spawnable name/model block is the same one Trusts use, so the client
  renders the pet and its name correctly.
- **Jug picker.**  The in-game HUD opens a jug list from the **Call Beast** and **Bestial
  Loyalty** tiles.  It lists the jugs actually in the character's equippable bags, with
  the pet each one calls; a jug that has been spent disappears on the next refresh.
  Picking one wears it in the ammo slot first (the client validates the jug before it
  sends the ability), calls the ability once the client sees it, and lets the normal state
  set return afterwards; the phase engine re-asserts the jug in the action's packet batch.
- **Pet Commands dialog.**  The **Pet commands** tile opens the commands for the pet that
  is out.  It shows the current job pair's pet commands (Fight/Heel/Stay/Sic/Ready/
  Reward/Snarl/Spur/Run Wild), hides Leave and Sic for a jug pet, and lists only the called jug's
  Ready moves in a **Ready** section.  A charmed pet gets Leave and no jug moves.
  Clicking a move sends `/pet "<move>"`; the server's Ready damage is still the separate
  piece below.
- Job-point rows feeding pet stats: BST Pet Accuracy/Familiar/Pet Attack Speed/Ready,
  DRG Wyvern Max HP/Attr Increase, PUP Automaton Max HP/MP, SMN avatar accuracy/magic
  accuracy/physical and magic damage.
- **Reward/Repair/Spirit Link** heal the active pet; **Elemental Siphon** and **Mana Cede**
  restore MP; **Blood Pact Rage/Ward** act through the avatar.
- Pet support statuses: Unleash, Spur, Overdrive, Astral Flow, Astral Conduit, Avatar's
  Favor.

## Not yet built

- Astral Flow/Astral Conduit, Avatar's Favor, Elemental Siphon, Mana Cede and Blood Pact
  damage; avatars currently follow and auto-attack only.
- BST **Familiar, Snarl and Feral Howl**; the full CHR/level/charm-duration model; Reward's
  food consumption and full heal; Ready/Sic ability damage and pet enmity transfer.
  **Spur** and **Unleash** put up their status icons but do not change the pet's stats or
  TP moves yet.  Charm, Tame, Gauge, Leave and Run Wild have simple working versions; Reward
  is a flat 300 HP pet heal.  The Ready moves are listed and sent by the HUD, but the zone
  server does not resolve their damage/status yet.
- Wyvern breath/restoring breath, Spirit Link and Deep Breathing.
- Automaton frames, attachments, maneuver burden, Repair and Overdrive.
- Pet status icons and the full client-side pet-command submenu.

Those systems are required before the remaining pet-specific job-point rows can be
applied. This foundation is deliberately limited to the shared follow/attack model.
