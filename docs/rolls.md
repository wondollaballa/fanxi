# Corsair rolls

Fanxi has a small Corsair roll system built on the normal status-effect path.

## What exists

- Fourteen job rolls get a client status icon and apply their modifier for the rolled
  value: Fighter's, Monk's, Healer's, Wizard's, Warlock's, Rogue's, Gallant's, Chaos,
  Hunter's, Samurai, Ninja, Magus's, Bolter's and Caster's.
- Roll value 1-6; **Snake Eye** makes the next roll an 11.
- **Double-Up** increments the active roll; **Fold** removes it.
- **Phantom Roll Duration** job-point row adds +40 s, giving a 100 s roll.
- The status icon, chat line (`Fighter's Roll: 6`) and modifiers are verified in game.

## Not yet built

- Wild Card, Cutting Cards, Quick Draw shots, Random Deal, Triple Shot, Optimal Range,
  Bust Evasion, ammunition consumption and the pet/avatar rolls.
- The remaining 8 COR category rows are listed in `docs/job-points.md`.
