# Dancer

Fanxi has a basic Dancer step/flourish layer.

## What exists

- **Steps**: Quickstep, Box Step, Stutter Step and Feather Step apply their debuff and
  build a **Finishing Move** (1–6), with the Step Duration job-point row extending the
  move to 100 s.
- **Flourishes**: Animated Flourish deals finishing-move-scaled damage, Reverse Flourish
  restores TP, Building Flourish puts up its status; the other flourishes consume a move.
- **No Foot Rise** grants three finishing moves.
- **Spectral Jig** grants Sneak and Invisible.
- **Trance, Grand Pas, Saber Dance, Fan Dance and Contradance** put up their statuses.
- Samba statuses and Waltz potency were already implemented.

## Still missing

- Jig Duration, Flourish I/II/III effects, Presto, full flourish debuffs and the complete
  Dancer job-point rows.
